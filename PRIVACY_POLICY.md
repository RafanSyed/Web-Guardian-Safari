# Privacy Policy for Web Guardian iOS / Pure Path

Last updated: September 18, 2026

## Overview
Web Guardian iOS (and its accompanying Safari Web Extension, Pure Path) respects your privacy. This application is designed to operate completely locally on your device.

## Data Collection and Storage
- **No Personal Data Collected:** Web Guardian iOS does not collect, track, or transmit any personally identifiable information, browsing history, or user data.
- **Local Processing:** All content filtering and URL analysis occur locally within the Safari browser on your iOS device. 
- **No External Servers:** No data or browsing activity is ever sent to external servers, third-party analytics services, or advertising networks.

## Third-Party Services
Web Guardian iOS does not integrate any third-party advertising SDKs, tracking tools, or external analytics frameworks.

## Contact
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us via the GitHub issue tracker for this repository.
